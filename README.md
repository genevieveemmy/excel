## 📋 Project Overview

This project delivers a comprehensive data analytics and AI implementation solution to empower management with executive insights, automated reporting, and operational optimization[cite: 1].

Key Datasets & Relational Schema
The analytics infrastructure integrates 9 core relational tables linked via a star-schema architecture:

Orders & Order Items: Core transactional fact tables capturing 50,000 orders.

Customers: Demographic records for 5,000 registered shoppers across Bronze, Silver, and Gold membership tiers.

Products & Inventory: Master catalog of 500 SKUs and real-time stock-on-hand tracking across 25 store branches.

Stores & Employees: Branch network mapping across Nigerian states with workforce allocations.

Returns & Suppliers: Quality assurance records (2,500 returns) and vendor supply chains.

🔑 Key Findings & Business Insights
Top-Line Performance: Generated ₦7,575,250,086.00 in total revenue across 50,000 transactions for the 2025 fiscal year.

Category Dominance: Beauty (₦1.76B) and Groceries (₦1.58B) lead top-line revenue, accounting for over 44% of total sales.

Regional Distribution: The South (₦2.42B) and East (₦2.12B) regions drive the majority of enterprise revenue.

Store Highlights: ShopSmart Lagos leads store performance with ₦1,232,190,000 in revenue.

Inventory Alert: Northern branches such as ShopSmart Enugu and ShopSmart Kano exhibit critically low stock levels (3,349 and 4,708 units respectively), requiring immediate restock intervention.
